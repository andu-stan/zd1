# AAC-LC decoder (fixed point)

Our decoder for what AirPlay 2 sends for music (buffered audio, an iPhone's mode): AAC-LC, one or two channels,
7.35-96 kHz, raw access units in, 16-bit PCM out. It replaces FDK in `zd1-airplay`: FDK's licence is not
GPL-compatible, it is 800 KB of the receiver for a profile we use a sliver of, and this one is faster on the
ARM926. Integer only, no division in the per-sample paths, so the same C builds for Warp0 (next).

| File | |
|---|---|
| `aacdec.c`, `aacdec.h` | the decoder (~700 lines): bitstream, Huffman, scale factors, pulses, TNS, PNS, mid/side and intensity stereo, dequantization, windows (sine/KBD; long, start, short, stop); the IMDCT is module/codecs/dsp's (shared with module/codecs/vorbis) |
| `aac_tables.c`, `aac_tables.h` | tables, generated by `tools/gentables.py` from the standard's codebooks and bands (as FFmpeg's `aactab.c` transcribes them) and computed windows; committed |
| `rust/` | crate `zd1-aac`: the decoder for Rust and a shairplay-rust `Decoder` for AAC streams (`zd1-warp0` hands it what Warp0 doesn't take) |
| `check/` | `aaccheck.c`, over raw frames (`tools/m4a2frames.py`): `dump` (the PCM, the frames refused and the tools used), `cmp` (against a reference decoder's PCM), `fuzz` (mutated frames under ASan/UBSan), `bench` (speed on the unit, `-DPROF` with the sampling profiler, `module/os/debug/prof.c`), `wav` (synthetic test signals for an encoder); the IMDCT's own check is in module/codecs/dsp |

## Numbers
Spectra are int32 Q2 (sign * |q|^(4/3) * 2^((sf - 100) / 4) * 4, eight times full scale before they saturate).
The IMDCT (module/codecs/dsp) is an N/4-point complex FFT between pre- and post-twiddles, in block floating point: each
block is normalised to below 2^27, the FFT scales by 1/N (radix-4 stages: Q30 twiddles and a high-word multiply are
the /4), each twiddled component is one rounded 64-bit multiply-accumulate. Time samples are PCM Q9 out of the IMDCT, Q8 after
the window (Q31, high word); the overlap is kept in Q8. Long blocks fold the IMDCT's reorder, both window halves and
the overlap-add into one pass.

## Checked [2026-10-24]
- **Against FDK** (configured as the receiver used it: limiter off; the comparison left with FDK): streams from FDK's encoder
  (music, transients, noise, wide stereo, sweep, quiet, near full scale; 24-128 kb/s, stereo and mono) and from
  Apple's encoder (`afconvert`, what iPhones use; 32-256 kb/s, 12 / 44.1 / 48 kHz): no frame rejected; maximum
  difference 1-4 LSB (4 only within a few dB of full scale), RMS 0.15-0.9 LSB, about two roundings' worth.
- **Against FFmpeg's float decoder** (a third opinion where FDK and we differ): near full scale we are within 2 LSB
  where FDK is 4 off; on the benchmark file no frame differs by more than 2 LSB, while FDK differs on 13 of 1,293.
  Noise substitution: band energies within 0.01 dB of FFmpeg's (FDK's high bands are up to 1.1 dB above both).
- **Tools exercised** (counters in the check build): long/start/short/stop windows, both window shapes, TNS (long
  and short), mid/side (per band and full), intensity stereo (in-phase and out-of-phase, thousands of bands in
  Apple's 64-256 kb/s streams), noise substitution, escape codes, channel pairs with separate windows, mono.
  Not exercised: pulse data (neither encoder emits it).
- **Robustness**: ~17,000 mutated frames (bit flips, truncation, random bytes) under ASan/UBSan: no fault.
- **On the unit**: 14.8 % of the ARM926 for 233 kb/s stereo (`aaccheck bench`; FDK 18.5 % with its limiter off,
  24 % with its defaults); the buffered AAC stream through `zd1-airplay` 23.9 % of the ARM (FDK: 27.3 %),
  29.3 % while the sender streams ahead (32 %), no xruns.

## On Warp0
The same C runs on Warp0 as its own firmware image (`module/warp0/fw/aac_main.c`, `w0aac.ld`): code and cold tables
from SDRAM through the I-cache, the working set and the per-frame tables (`AAC_FAST`) in local RAM, the overlap and
the start/short/stop buffer in SDRAM (`AAC_EXT_BUFFERS`). Output bit-identical to the ARM's, 1,293 frames,
7.0 ms of Warp0 per stereo frame (30 % of its time). Through `zd1-airplay` (frames
submitted ahead of playout, `module/warp0/rust`): the buffered AAC stream takes 11 % of the ARM (24 % decoding
on the ARM), the PCM bit-identical over a million samples [HW 2026-10-24].

Portability rules the Warp0 build taught: everything `zero()` clears by words must be word aligned (an unaligned
word store on Warp0 silently drops the low address bits and writes the wrong word, `docs/module/warp.md` section 3;
on the ARM the kernel traps and fixes it up); no byte-field copies the compiler can merge into unaligned word moves
(`ICS_INFO_WORDS`). `module/warp0/tools/emu.py aac` runs the firmware under Unicorn on the host against the host
build.

## Building and running the checks
```
cc -O2 -DAAC_STATS -I. -I../dsp -o /tmp/aaccheck check/aaccheck.c aacdec.c aac_tables.c ../dsp/imdct.c \
   ../dsp/imdct_tables.c -lm
python3 -I tools/m4a2frames.py FILE.m4a FILE.frames
/tmp/aaccheck dump FILE.frames OUT.raw [CH] [RATE]
```
`check/aaccheck.c` has every command and the fuzzer's and the unit's build lines in its first lines.
